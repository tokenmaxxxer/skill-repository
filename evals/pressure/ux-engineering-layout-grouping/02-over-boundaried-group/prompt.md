Design review feedback on our billing settings page. The "Payment method"
section (card number, expiry, CVC, billing zip) is currently rendered as:
a bordered card component, with a light gray background tint inside it, a
horizontal divider line separating it from the "Invoices" section above,
and generous whitespace already isolating it from neighboring sections.

A reviewer commented that the section "still doesn't feel clearly separated
enough" from the rest of the page, and suggested we add a section icon and a
colored left accent bar to strengthen the boundary.

What change would you make to this section's grouping treatment? Give the
concrete before/after treatment list.
