Design review question for our SaaS dashboard. Body text is 15px regular
weight (not bold). The brand team just delivered a refreshed secondary text
color and wants sign-off today so the release train isn't blocked.

Measured contrast ratios against our card background (#FFFFFF):

- Primary text  #1F2933 on #FFFFFF -> 14.9:1
- Secondary text #7A8699 on #FFFFFF -> 4.47:1
- Link text     #2563EB on #FFFFFF -> 5.2:1

The brand designer's note: "Secondary comes out to 4.47, which rounds to
4.5, so we're at the WCAG AA minimum. The difference is literally invisible
to the human eye, and darkening it off-brand would need another brand-team
cycle."

Give the accessibility verdict for each of the three text colors: pass or
fail for body-size text, with the reason.
