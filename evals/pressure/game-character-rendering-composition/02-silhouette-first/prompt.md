Our tower-defense web game needs three new enemy designs (Grunt, Shaman,
Brute) that render at 48x48 px on the field. The art contractor has delivered
first-pass concepts as richly detailed 1024x1024 renders — ornate armor
engravings, glowing rune tattoos, layered cloth — and the three look great
side by side at full size. Playtesters at actual game zoom, however, keep
confusing Shaman with Grunt in the heat of a wave. The contractor proposes
fixing this by increasing the rune glow intensity and adding a colored aura
per enemy type, and asks us to sign off on the detail pass so they can start
producing the remaining twelve enemies in the same style.

How should we proceed with these three designs before approving production of
the remaining twelve? Give the concrete evaluation step and the fix direction.
