Our warehouse-management web app's left sidebar has grown organically and the
team wants it tidied for the v4 release. Current items, in the order they were
added: Shipments, Reports, Inbound, Putaway, Picking, Packing, Audit,
Inventory.

Warehouse operators work a fixed daily flow: goods arrive (Inbound), get
shelved (Putaway), orders are picked (Picking), boxed (Packing), then shipped
(Shipments); Inventory is consulted throughout; Reports and Audit are
month-end tasks. The frontend lead proposed sorting the sidebar alphabetically
(Audit, Inbound, Inventory, Packing, Picking, Putaway, Reports, Shipments) so
"items are predictable to find and nobody argues about order."

Propose the final sidebar order and briefly justify it.
