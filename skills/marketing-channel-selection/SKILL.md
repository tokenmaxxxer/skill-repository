# Channel selection and budget allocation

Research trail: Growth Method's B2B channel-strategy framework, Belkins'
2026 B2B-channel-effectiveness study, INFUSE's channel-orchestration
guide, and Cometly's/Prescient AI's 2026 budget-allocation guides, all
fetched this session.

## Rules

1. When selecting a channel, verify the target segment actually spends
   time there before allocating budget — you cannot generate demand on
   a channel your audience does not use, regardless of that channel's
   average ROI elsewhere. source:
   https://growthmethod.com/marketing-channel-strategy/

2. For a B2B target segment, prefer channels built around
   firmographic/professional targeting (LinkedIn, industry events,
   email) over broad consumer channels, and for a B2C segment prefer
   social, paid search, and mobile-app channels, since channel ROI
   differs sharply by audience type (LinkedIn ads: 229% ROI for B2B vs
   57% for B2C). source: https://belkins.io/blog/b2b-marketing-channels

3. When choosing between two channels with similar reach, pick the one
   backed by quarterly-refreshed audience-preference data over one
   chosen from a prior cycle's assumption, since audience channel
   preference shifts and a stale channel mix underperforms a
   re-validated one. source:
   https://infuse.com/insight/5-steps-orchestrate-marketing-channels-demand-generation/

4. When a channel's incremental ROI or ROAS falls behind other channels
   in the mix over a full attribution cycle, move the marginal budget
   dollar toward the higher-incremental channel in 5-10% increments
   rather than reallocating the full budget at once, so the impact can
   be measured before committing further. source:
   https://www.cometly.com/post/wasting-budget-on-wrong-channels

5. **REMOVAL**: When a channel is confirmed underperforming across a
   full attribution cycle (not one campaign) with no evidence it powers
   upstream performance elsewhere, cut it from the active mix rather
   than leaving it funded "to be safe" — top teams reallocate at least
   10-15% of budget every quarter on exactly this trigger. source:
   https://www.cometly.com/post/marketing-budget-allocation-across-channels

6. Before cutting an underperforming channel, check whether it is
   powering conversions attributed to a better-performing channel
   downstream, and if so keep a minimum-viable spend there instead of a
   full cut — apparent underperformance on paper can mask essential
   upstream work. source:
   https://prescientai.com/blog/how-to-allocate-marketing-budget-across-channels
