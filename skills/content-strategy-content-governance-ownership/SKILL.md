# Content governance and ownership

Issue: tokenmaxxxer/skill-repository#82 (professional-discipline gap #4,
content-strategy family). Research trail:
docs/issue-82/reports/content-design/survey.md, Angle 3 (content
governance/ownership models: RACI, centralized/decentralized/hybrid).

## Trigger

Apply this skill when assigning ownership for a content domain,
resolving who has final sign-off on a content decision, or choosing
between a centralized, decentralized, or hybrid governance model for
content across teams.

## Procedure

1. When a content domain has zero or multiple final approvers, assign
   exactly one Accountable owner before anything else (rule 1).
2. When choosing a governance model, pick it by organizational scale
   and update frequency, not default inertia (rule 2).

## Output shape

A governance decision: the domain affected, the applicable rule
number, and the action (name the single Accountable owner, or pick
centralized/decentralized/hybrid with the reason).

## Rules

1. Apply RACI to content ownership: Responsible (executes), Accountable
   (final sign-off), Consulted (subject-matter input before
   completion), Informed (notified after, cannot block) — "make sure
   there is only one A for each role." Where an audit or calendar
   surfaces a content domain with zero or multiple Accountable owners,
   that is the governance defect to fix before any other content
   decision on that domain proceeds; multiple A's break sign-off
   clarity. source: https://contentstrategyinc.com/how-to-use-a-raci-chart-to-define-content-roles-and-responsibilities/

2. Choose the governance model by organizational scale and update
   frequency, not default inertia: centralized (one team controls all
   content decisions) when one team can still keep up with volume
   without becoming a bottleneck; decentralized (each team governs its
   own) when domains are independent enough to not need cross-team
   consistency; hybrid (central standards, team-level execution) as the
   default fit for a growing organization that needs both consistency
   and throughput. source: https://thestacc.com/blog/content-governance-guide/
